GoHighLevel · for every business

One system for leads, follow-up, booking and reviews

GoHighLevel can replace five or six separate tools. I set it up so leads from every channel land in one pipeline, get followed up automatically by SMS and email, book themselves on a calendar, and leave a review after the job is done.

Businesses I set it up for

  • Real estate
  • Investors & wholesalers
  • Lenders
  • Home services
  • Clinics & med spas
  • Coaches & consultants
  • Marketing agencies
  • Local businesses

What I build in GoHighLevel

Account & sub-account setup

Sub-accounts, users, permissions, phone numbers, email domains and snapshots for fast repeat setups.

Pipelines & opportunities

Stages that match how the business sells, with automatic stage moves as leads respond or book.

Workflows & triggers

Form submitted, tag added, stage changed, appointment booked or no-show: each one starts the right actions.

Lead routing

Round-robin or rule-based assignment by source, service or location, with instant team notifications.

Multi-step follow-up sequences

SMS, email and call-task cadences for lead nurturing that stop automatically when the lead replies.

Campaigns & re-engagement

Broadcasts and long-term drip campaigns that wake up old and cold leads.

Calendars & reminders

Booking calendars with confirmations, reminders and no-show recovery to protect show rates.

Funnels, websites & forms

Landing pages, lead forms and surveys that write straight into custom fields.

Missed-call text back

Every missed call gets an instant text so the lead doesn't call the next company.

Reviews & reputation

Automatic review requests after a job or appointment, with alerts for unhappy replies.

AI chat & conversation bots

Bots that answer FAQs, qualify leads and book appointments on website chat, SMS and social.

Integrations & webhooks

Meta, Twilio, Zapier and n8n through native integrations, inbound webhooks and the API.
