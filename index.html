Zapier · integrations

Connecting CRMs to every lead source

I build Zaps that move leads and data between your tools automatically: lead sources into the CRM, CRM into dialers, SMS tools and e-signature, and results back again. Each Zap replaces a manual copy-paste step.

CRMs I connect

  • Follow Up Boss
  • GoHighLevel
  • HubSpot
  • Salesforce
  • Pipedrive
  • Airtable
  • ClickUp

What I do in Zapier

Multi-step Zaps

One trigger, many actions: create the lead, tag it, assign it, notify the team and log it.

Filters & Paths

If-this-then-that logic that sends each lead down the right path by source, stage or answer.

Formatter

Clean phone numbers, names, dates and text so data lands in the CRM correctly.

Field mapping & lookup tables

Map fields between different CRMs and tools, and translate values like stages and owners.

Find-or-create

Search first, then update or create, so the same person never becomes two contacts.

Webhooks

Catch data from any tool that can send a webhook, and send data to tools with no Zapier app.

Email Parser

Pull lead details out of notification emails from sources that have no integration.

Delays & schedules

Wait-then-check steps, such as "no call logged in 5 minutes, re-assign the lead".

Zapier Tables & Storage

Keep counters, round-robin positions and lookup data between Zap runs.

AI by Zapier

AI steps that summarize messages, classify replies and extract details from text.

Monitoring & fixes

Watch live Zaps, fix failed runs and add error alerts so no lead is lost.

Task cost cleanup

Rebuild heavy Zaps with filters and fewer steps to lower monthly Zapier task usage.
